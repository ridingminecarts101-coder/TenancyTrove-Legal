Privacy, in plain language.

TenancyTrove is a local-first rental record. Your tenancy entries and attached file bytes are stored locally on this device in the app or browser profile, not in a TenancyTrove account.

What the app stores

Addresses, dates, contacts, notes, amounts, file names, selected file bytes and metadata you enter or attach are stored using on-device IndexedDB, including in the installed iOS app. A local preference records when you last prepared a ZIP backup. The PWA caches app code and a report font for offline use; the iOS app bundles those assets.

The current app has no account, cloud sync, analytics, advertising, or automatic evidence upload. RL Applications does not receive your tenancy records through the app.

What can leave your device

When you click an official help link, your browser contacts that external website under its own privacy policy. If you use the PWA, its website host receives ordinary web requests and may retain technical access logs. The installed iOS app loads its core assets locally. If you choose to email support, the message and any attachment you send go to the email provider and RL Applications; the app does not attach your records automatically.

Exports and device access

ZIP backups and PDF summaries are created on your device. The PWA downloads them through your browser; the iOS app opens the system share sheet so you can save them to Files or another destination. The iOS app uses a temporary cache copy for sharing and attempts to remove it after the sheet closes; the device may also clear its cache. TenancyTrove does not encrypt its on-device database or ZIP/PDF exports. Anyone able to access your unlocked device, browser profile or exports may be able to read them. A device backup or sync service you choose may copy downloaded files. Store backups where only you can access them. If evidence collection could put you at risk, see eSafety’s evidence-safety guidance.

Retention and deletion

Records remain in the app or browser profile until you delete a record or tenancy, remove the app, clear site data, or the device removes storage. Export regularly; an app backup stored only on the same device may also be lost with that device. Deleting inside the app does not erase ZIP/PDF copies, browser backups, or files you saved elsewhere. To clear all current PWA data, use your browser’s site-data controls for TenancyTrove. For the installed iOS app, delete tenancies inside the app or remove the app to clear its local container. We cannot recover a lost local record without your own export.

Questions and changes

Email help.rlapplications@gmail.com. Please do not include sensitive evidence unless you intend to send it. We will update this page if the app adds cloud, account or analytics features before enabling them.

About the tool: TenancyTrove does not submit official reports, deliver legal notices, or provide legal advice. Check current state or territory guidance for formal obligations.
