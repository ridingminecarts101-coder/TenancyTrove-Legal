Help with TenancyTrove.

A practical guide to your private rental record.

Start a tenancy

Choose New tenancy, enter a street address and state or territory, then save. Use New record for condition notes, repairs, communications, payments, documents or general notes. You can attach files up to about 50 MB each; TenancyTrove does not prove where or when they originated.

Keep a backup

Open Backup & restore and export a full ZIP. Verify it is saved somewhere you can access, and test importing it on another device or in a separate browser profile. The ZIP includes selected file bytes and integrity checks. Imports add missing records and files while keeping matching existing items unchanged; conflicting IDs are rejected. Existing records are not overwritten. The PDF is a readable summary and is not a restorable backup. Neither export is encrypted.

Offline use

The installed iOS app bundles its core assets for offline use. In a supported browser, open the PWA online first so its core recording and PDF tools can be cached for offline use. Official help sites need a connection. Local storage is device-managed and can be removed if you delete the app, clear browser site data or run into storage pressure.

Rental law and disputes

Use Rental help in the app to choose your state or territory and open current government and consumer authority guidance about condition reports, repairs, bonds and disputes. TenancyTrove does not submit forms, send notices, lodge bond claims or determine a deadline. If something is urgent, use your authority’s current instructions or seek qualified help.

Privacy and safety

Read the privacy page before storing sensitive material. On a shared device, another person with access to your unlocked device, browser profile or exports may be able to read them. eSafety explains safer evidence collection.

Contact RL Applications

For product problems or a correction to a help link, email help.rlapplications@gmail.com. Include your browser and a description of the issue. Please do not send an agreement, ID, photos, or other private evidence unless you specifically want RL Applications to receive it. We cannot access or restore data stored only in your app or browser profile.
